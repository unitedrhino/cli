# system/notify/notification

> 该 group 共 9 个端点。

- `POST /api/v1/system/notify/notification/copy` [platform] 复制手动通知
- `POST /api/v1/system/notify/notification/create` [platform] 创建手动通知
- `POST /api/v1/system/notify/notification/delete` [platform] 删除手动通知
- `POST /api/v1/system/notify/notification/estimate-users` [platform] 预估送达用户数
- `POST /api/v1/system/notify/notification/index` [platform] 获取手动通知列表
- `POST /api/v1/system/notify/notification/read` [platform] 获取手动通知详情
- `POST /api/v1/system/notify/notification/revoke` [platform] 撤回手动通知
- `POST /api/v1/system/notify/notification/send` [platform] 发送手动通知
- `POST /api/v1/system/notify/notification/update` [platform] 更新手动通知
