# things/product/remoteConfig

> 该 group 共 4 个端点。

- `POST /api/v1/things/product/remote-config/create` [admin] 创建配置
- `POST /api/v1/things/product/remote-config/get-list` [admin] 获取配置列表
- `POST /api/v1/things/product/remote-config/lastest-read` [admin] 获取最新配置
- `POST /api/v1/things/product/remote-config/push-all` [admin] 推送配置
