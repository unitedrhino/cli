# system/agreement

> 该 group 共 6 个端点。

- `POST /api/v1/system/agreement/create` [platform] 创建协议
- `POST /api/v1/system/agreement/delete` [platform] 删除协议
- `POST /api/v1/system/agreement/get-list` [platform] 获取协议列表
- `POST /api/v1/system/agreement/get-one` [platform] 获取协议详情
- `POST /api/v1/system/agreement/preview-render` [platform] 预览渲染协议
- `POST /api/v1/system/agreement/update` [platform] 更新协议
