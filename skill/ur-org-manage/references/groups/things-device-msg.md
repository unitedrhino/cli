# things/device/msg

> 该 group 共 17 个端点。

- `POST /api/v1/things/device/msg/abnormal-log/get-list` [admin] 获取设备异常日志
- `POST /api/v1/things/device/msg/event-log/get-list` [admin] 获取事件历史记录
- `POST /api/v1/things/device/msg/gateway-can-bind/get-list` [admin] 获取网关可以绑定的子设备列表
- `POST /api/v1/things/device/msg/hub-log/get-list` [admin] 获取云端诊断日志
- `POST /api/v1/things/device/msg/property-agg/by-device/get-list` [admin] 弃用
- `POST /api/v1/things/device/msg/property-agg/get-list` [admin] 弃用
- `POST /api/v1/things/device/msg/property-latest-agg/get-list` [admin] 聚合属性最新值
- `POST /api/v1/things/device/msg/property-latest/get-list` [admin] 获取最新属性记录
- `POST /api/v1/things/device/msg/property-log-agg/by-device/get-list` [admin] 聚合属性历史记录,设备维度
- `POST /api/v1/things/device/msg/property-log-agg/get-list` [admin] 聚合属性历史记录
- `POST /api/v1/things/device/msg/property-log-latest/get-list` [admin] 弃用
- `POST /api/v1/things/device/msg/property-log/batch-get-list` [admin] 批量获取单个id属性历史记录
- `POST /api/v1/things/device/msg/property-log/get-list` [admin] 获取单个id属性历史记录
- `POST /api/v1/things/device/msg/sdk-log/get-list` [admin] 获取设备sdk日志
- `POST /api/v1/things/device/msg/send-log/get-list` [admin] 获取设备命令日志
- `POST /api/v1/things/device/msg/shadow/get-list` [admin] 获取设备影子列表
- `POST /api/v1/things/device/msg/status-log/get-list` [admin] 获取设备状态日志
